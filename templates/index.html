About This Exhibit

Recognition is performed entirely in your browser using a pretrained MobileNet vision model (TensorFlow.js) — no images are uploaded to a server. Every note you hear is synthesized live with the Web Audio API, tuned to the real pitches of each instrument's strings, keys, or reeds.

The Instrument Gallery — a self-contained exhibit. No server-side ML, no audio files, runs entirely offline after first load.
